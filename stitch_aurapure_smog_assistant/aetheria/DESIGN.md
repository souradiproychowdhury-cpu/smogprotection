## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an aesthetic of regal atmospheric majesty, blending high-end aristocratic opulence with cutting-edge computational intelligence. Designed for high-net-worth individuals, urban executives, and discerning wellness connoisseurs facing severe winter smog and industrial particulate crises, the interface conveys impenetrable sanctuary, absolute environmental command, and bespoke calm.

The visual direction unifies **Glassmorphic Sovereignty** with **Tactile Gilded Luxury**:
- Pure crystalline frosted-glass surfaces suspended over deep cosmic voids.
- Subtle liquid-aurora refractions behind key dynamic layers, representing purified airflow and environmental clarity.
- Gilded filigree accents and luminous champagne rim-lights evoking bespoke horology and imperial navigational instruments.
- High-contrast visual hierarchies designed to pierce through haze, transforming complex atmospheric sensor data and GenAI foresight into an effortless, serene ritual.

## Colors
The palette balances deep midnight abysses with lustrous jewel tones and warm metallic illumination:

- **Primary (`#F59E0B` - Gilded Champagne Gold):** Denotes supreme prestige, high-priority AI insights, active regal CTAs, and protective atmospheric boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#059669` - Lustrous Royal Emerald / Teal):** Represents absolute purity, pristine oxygen levels, purified sanctuaries, and optimal microclimates.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8` - Glacial Aether):** Evokes crisp alpine atmosphere, ionizer status, fine particulate (PM2.5) dispersion gradients, and predictive airflow trajectories.
- **Neutral (`#0B1120` - Royal Midnight Sapphire):** Deep, impenetrable cosmic foundation that eliminates blue-light glare and maximizes the brilliance of frosted glass tiers and gilded halos.

### Functional Palette Nuances
- **Substrate Tiers:** Base backdrop operates at `#070A12` with gradient undercurrents surging to `#0F172A` and imperial `#1E1B4B`.
- **AQI Atmospheric Alert Spectrum:**
  - *Sovereign Pristine (0-25 AQI):* `#059669` with a subtle `#34D399` halo.
  - *Temperate Shift (26-75 AQI):* `#10B981` transitioning into soft cyan `#06B6D4`.
  - *Smog Intrusion (76-150 AQI):* Warm Imperial Amber `#F59E0B` with high metallic sheen.
  - *Hazardous Sovereign Breach (151+ AQI):* Intense Damson Crimson `#E11D48` encased in gold hazard pinstripes.

## Typography
The typographic rhythm bridges editorial luxury with clinical clarity.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Imparts heritage, high culture, and ceremonial gravitas. Used for macroscopic air quality summaries, generative briefing titles, and atmospheric sanctuary narratives. It utilizes italicized emphasis sparingly on key status words (e.g., *Purified*, *Hazardous*, *Sanctuary*) to inject bespoke human discernment.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides an architectural, razor-sharp geometric counterpart. The tall x-height and crisp apertures guarantee immediate legibility through variable lighting and high-contrast ambient conditions.
- **Labeling & Metadata:** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to simulate high-precision flight and nautical dials.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a responsive, fluid grid engineered for breathing room and intentional restraint. High atmospheric particulate matter causes sensory fatigue; the digital environment directly counteracts this with deliberate structural calm and wide negative spaces.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout flanked by generous `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters, constraining dashboards to a max-width of 1440px to ensure panoramic balance.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and condensed `1rem` gutters for effortless single-handed navigation.
- **Micro-Spacers:** Component internals leverage an 8-point baseline rhythm, using `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tight telemetry arrays, and `space-xl` to detach GenAI coaching dialogues from raw metric monitors.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created without standard drop shadows, relying instead on frosted-glass translucency, internal refraction lines, and chromatic backlights:

- **Surface Tiers (Glassmorphism):**
  - *Tier 0 (Substrate Canvas):* Deep gradient wash between `#070A12` and `#0F172A`.
  - *Tier 1 (Atmospheric Base Plate):* Fill of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)` with `24px` backdrop-filter blur.
  - *Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Vault):* Fill of `rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.45)` with `32px` blur and an inner specular stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Gilded Rim Lighting & Outlines:**
  - Key focus states and elevated GenAI cards feature a dual border: an inner 1px gold gradient line (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.6), rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1))`) accompanied by an outer micro-glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 25px -5px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`).
- **Atmospheric Backlights:**
  - High-risk smog anomalies cast an ambient, ultra-diffuse backlight aura (80px blur, 15% opacity) radiating directly from behind the primary AQI dial.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy merges the precision geometry of royal cut gems with the soft curvature of fluid aerodynamic vessels:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to compact inputs, actionable chips, and telemetry data-pills.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Standard curvature for primary frosted-glass containers, GenAI briefing modules, and diagnostic charts.
- **Sanctuary Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for large hero viewports, master ambient dials, and modal dialogs.
- **Filigree Accent Geometry:** Decorative micro-elements utilize chamfered corner cuts at 45-degree angles on top-right edges of primary badges to echo luxury chronometer bevels.

## Components

### Buttons & Atmospheric Controls
- **Primary Imperial CTA:** Solid Gilded Champagne gradient (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`) with obsidian typography (`#0B1120`), high-weight font (`600`), and a directional top highlight mimicking hand-polished gold. Hover state increases peripheral outer luminescence (`0 0 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Translucent deep sapphire base (`rgba(30, 27, 75, 0.6)`) with a 1px frosted champagne border, soft white text, and a crisp hover lift (+1px Y-axis translation, gold opacity elevation).
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Pure typography with an animated gilded underline that expands outward from the center on focus.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed sapphire cavities (`rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.85)`) framed by a subtle 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- Active/focused state reveals an inner emerald-to-gold chromatic gradient hairline along the bottom edge, complemented by an ambient, non-intrusive inner glow.
- Placeholder text utilizes subdued celestial silver (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.4)`).

### Atmospheric Data Cards & AI Panels
- Rendered in high-viscosity frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`).
- Headers pair an editorial Playfair Display title with a monospaced Plus Jakarta Sans timestamp or localized particulate badge.
- Cards host a micro-sheen on their top-left corner, giving an impression of backlit optical crystal.

### Chips & Sensor Badges
- Encapsulated pills featuring an integrated pulsating status bead (e.g., emerald green for HEPA active, amber for smog alert).
- Borders use dynamic opacity: 20% default, transitioning to 80% with a matching color-matched shadow when the sensor reports critical microclimate variations.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Micro-Switches
- Custom tactile jewel switches: Unchecked states sit flush with the dark substrate; checked states snap forward with an authentic metallic gold bezel and luminous teal core.

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **The Royal AQI Crown Dial:** An interactive multi-layered gauge displaying real-time PM2.5, PM10, and Ozone concentrations, ringed with dual metallic tick marks and continuous liquid-fluid movement representing indoor air displacement.
- **GenAI Mitigation Oracle:** An conversational luxury assistant module framed by subtle emerald-gold aurora filaments, outputting synthesized mitigation maneuvers (e.g., HVAC pressurization, hyper-localized travel paths, ionizer scheduling) in balanced serif-sans typography.
